# Changelog

## 1.3.1
* Guide book: entry titles now wrap only between words. A title whose longest word does not fit next to the icon (for example the Polish "Blok netherowych brodawek") is written under the icon across the page instead of being cut mid-word. The text check now fails on any word that would be cut, in every title, heading and page, in English and Polish.

## 1.3.0
* Added a Nether Wart Block → 9 Nether Wart back recipe.

## 1.2.0
* Added the Storage & Back Guide, crafted shapeless from 1 Book and 1 Flint Block. Right-click opens a read-only illustrated guidebook (two-page spread, clickable categories and entries, and visible crafting grids with item icons) covering every storage block, the back recipes, and the config toggles, in English and Polish.

## 1.1.0
* Added sandstone → 4 sand and red sandstone → 4 red sand back recipes.
* The Ender Pearl Block is now a cluster of 9 touching pearls (8 at the corners and 1 in the centre) that you can see between.

## 1.0.0
* The 26.2 build requires Fabric Loader 0.19.5+ (not 0.20.1).
* First release for Minecraft 26.3 and 26.2 (Fabric).
* 13 storage blocks: Flint Block, Sugar Cane Bundle, Charcoal Block, Gunpowder Block, Blaze Rod Bundle, Leather Block, Feather Block, Ender Pearl Block, Rotten Flesh Block, Carrot / Potato / Beetroot / Apple Crate.
* 13 back recipes: Quartz, Amethyst, Clay, Glowstone, Snow, Honeycomb, Bricks, Nether Bricks, Dripstone, Prismarine, Prismarine Bricks, Dark Prismarine, Magma.
* Config (`config/storageback.json`, Mod Menu + Cloth Config screen): *Balancing changes* (amethyst and magma cream recipes) and *Back recipes*.
* English and Polish translations.
